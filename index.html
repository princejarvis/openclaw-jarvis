Your inbox and calendar, with a little more order.

OpenClaw Jarvis is a personal assistant that connects to Gmail and Google Calendar to help its operator manage email and plans.

How your data is handled

Email, on request.

Find relevant messages, summarize conversations, organize mail, and prepare or send replies when the operator requests them. Available actions depend on the permissions granted.

Calendar, on request.

Check upcoming plans, look up event details, and create or update events when requested. Calendar access is authorized through the operator’s Google account.

A personal connection, not a public service.

This website explains one personally operated installation. It does not offer account registration, collect Google credentials, or connect visitors’ accounts. The assistant runs separately from this informational site.

A personal OpenClaw installation. Not affiliated with Google.
